Nutrition Tips & Insights

Expert advice on healthy cooking practices and maximizing the nutritional value of your meals.

🌈

Eat the Rainbow

Different colored fruits and vegetables contain unique phytonutrients. Aim to include at least 3-5 different colors in your daily meals for optimal nutrition.

🥩

Prioritize Protein

Include a quality protein source at each meal to maintain muscle mass, boost metabolism, and keep you feeling satisfied longer.

🫒

Healthy Fats Matter

Don't fear fats! Olive oil, avocados, nuts, and fatty fish provide essential fatty acids that support brain health and nutrient absorption.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Proper hydration supports digestion, energy levels, and overall health. Aim for 8 glasses of water daily, more if you're active.

🥬

Fiber is Your Friend

High-fiber foods promote gut health, regulate blood sugar, and help maintain a healthy weight. Include whole grains, legumes, and vegetables daily.

🧂

Season Wisely

Use herbs, spices, citrus, and vinegars to add flavor without excess sodium. Many spices also offer additional health benefits.
